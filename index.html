01 · HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, all on your machine.

  1. your-photo.jpg

    1Drop a photo

    Pick a photo. Your browser reads it and shrinks it to 512 pixels; it never leaves this device.

  2. 32 128 512 2,048 8,000

    232 blobs become 8,000

    It starts with 32 big blobs and keeps adding smaller ones where the picture is still most wrong.

  3. 3Gradient descent

    Every step nudges each blob's place, size, angle, colour and opacity to make the mistake smaller.

02 · THE CODE

Your picture is a list of code.

Every blob is one line of hex. The text file is the picture: open it in the app and it paints itself again.

stage-08000.txt12 of 8,000 lines · select one to copy it

03 · THE POSTER

Print the code.

The poster is the whole code file, every blob line, tinted by your picture so the picture shows through the text.

Download it as vector SVG that stays sharp at any size, or as a 300 dpi PNG, from A4 up to 50 × 70 cm.

04 · HONEST AND PRIVATE

Nothing generated. Nothing sent.

  • No AI generation

    Nothing is invented. Every blob is fitted to your own photo by plain gradient descent: re-painted, not generated.

  • Your photo stays here

    It is read, fitted and drawn in this browser tab, on your own GPU. The site has no backend that could receive it.

  • No analytics, no tracking

    No analytics, no cookies, no tracking scripts. Nothing on this site is loaded from another site.

  • Open source, MIT

    Every line is on GitHub under the MIT licence: read it, run it, change it.
